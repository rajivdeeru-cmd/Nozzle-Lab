01 / CONTEXT

When air moves faster than sound, density becomes visible.

Supersonic flows contain shock waves, expansion fans and shear layers that cannot be observed with the naked eye. This project makes those structures measurable — first through Schlieren imaging, then through a computer vision pipeline designed to identify and quantify them.
